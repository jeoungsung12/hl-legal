Account Deletion

Haeryeong: Last Light · Operated by Jeong Seong-yun (individual developer)

This English version is a translation provided for convenience. If there is any conflict between the English and Korean versions, the Korean version prevails.

Delete in the Game (recommended)

  1. Open the Game and sign in.
  2. Tap Settings > Account > Delete Account.
  3. Read the notice and confirm; your account is deleted immediately. For security, you may be asked to sign in with Apple or Google once more.

If You Cannot Use the App

If you have removed the app or cannot launch it, send the following to onsaemigol@gmail.com. After verifying your identity, we will delete your account within 10 days and let you know the result.

  • Subject: Account deletion request
  • The sign-in method you used (Apple or Google) and the email address of that account (if you used Apple's "Hide My Email", the relay address)
  • Your in-game nickname (if you know it)

What Is Deleted

  • Account information: sign-in links, nickname, settings
  • All of your solo rooms, with their progress and inventory
  • Your member information and character status in co-op rooms, and your "Fellow Survivors" list
  • Ad viewing records and game files stored on your device
  • Online service connection information used for co-op connections

What Remains or Is Handled Separately

  • Co-op rooms: If you are the host and other members remain, host status passes to one of them and the room continues. If no members remain, the room is deleted as well.
  • Error logs and usage statistics: Kept in a form that cannot directly identify you, and deleted automatically after 90 days and 2 months respectively.
  • Backup copies: Overwritten and gone within 30 days at most.
  • Your Apple or Google account itself is not deleted. If you signed in with Apple, the link between the app and your Apple ID is also removed.

Deleted accounts and progress cannot be recovered.
