Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026 · Applies to: the mobile game Haeryeong: Last Light (the "Game")

This English version is a translation provided for convenience. If there is any conflict between the English and Korean versions, the Korean version prevails.

Jeong Seong-yun (individual developer) (the "Operator") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws, and processes the personal information of Game users as described below.

1. Purposes of Processing

  • Account verification and sign-in: identifying users who sign in with an Apple or Google account, so they can continue their progress even after changing devices. Guest (non-member) play is not offered.
  • Saving game progress: saving and loading progress, inventory, difficulty and settings for solo and co-op rooms.
  • Co-op play: finding rooms by invite code, connecting up to 3 users in real time, and providing a list of users you have played with ("Fellow Survivors").
  • Service stability: analysing the causes of errors and crashes, and adjusting feature settings remotely.
  • Usage statistics: producing statistics needed to improve the Game, such as the points in each stage where players stop. These are not used to identify individuals.
  • Advertising: showing rewarded ads when the user chooses to watch one, and granting the reward.

During co-op play, participants' devices may connect directly to each other to reduce latency. In that case, your IP address may be shared with the devices of other participants in the same room. If a direct connection is not possible, traffic goes through Epic Online Services relay servers.

3. Processing and Retention Periods

  • Account, profile and game data are kept until you delete your account and are destroyed immediately upon a deletion request. Backup copies are overwritten and disappear within 30 days at most.
  • A co-op room continues to exist while other members remain in it. The deleted user's identifier and character status are removed from the room. If the host deletes their account, host status passes to a remaining member; if no members remain, the room is deleted as well.
  • Error logs are deleted automatically 90 days after collection, and usage statistics 2 months after collection.
  • Where the law requires certain information to be preserved, it is stored separately for that period.

4. Provision to Third Parties

The Operator does not provide personal information to third parties, except where there is a legal basis or where an investigative authority requests it following procedures set out in law.

7. Relationship with Apple Inc. and Google LLC

When signing in, you authenticate directly on Apple's or Google's screen. The Operator does not receive your password. Processing of information in your Apple or Google account itself follows each company's policy. App installation and payments are handled by the App Store and Google Play.

8. Destruction Procedure and Method

  • When the retention period ends or you request account deletion, the information is destroyed without delay.
  • Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered, and game files stored on your device are removed together by the app's account deletion feature.

9. Rights of Data Subjects and How to Exercise Them

  • You may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information.
  • You can change your nickname and delete your account yourself in the Game under Settings > Account. If you cannot use the app, you can make a request using the method on the Account Deletion page.
  • For other requests, contact us at the address in Section 13 and we will tell you the result within 10 days.
  • A legal representative or an authorised person may also make a request on your behalf, in which case a power of attorney must be submitted.

10. Security Measures

  • We apply encryption in transit (TLS) and access control for stored data (database security rules that allow access only to the authenticated user and room members).
  • Server secrets are not stored in the source code repository and are managed separately.
  • The number of people who handle personal information is kept to a minimum.

11. Automatic Collection Tools: Use and Opting Out

  • The Game does not use web cookies. It uses an app instance identifier (for statistics) and an advertising identifier (for ads).
  • iOS: The first time ads are prepared, you are asked whether to allow app tracking. If you decline, you can still use the Game and rewarded ads, and you will see non-personalised ads. You can change this at any time under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: You can delete or reset your Advertising ID under Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Personalised ads are not used in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland.

12. Children's Personal Information

The Game is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that such information has been collected, we delete it immediately. The Game's age rating follows the rating shown on the app store.
